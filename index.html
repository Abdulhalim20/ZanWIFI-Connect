ZanWIFI Connect Platform

Build Your Own WiFi Business in Zanzibar and East Africa

Already have internet? Don't let it sit idle — ZanWIFI Connect turns your existing connection into a profitable hotspot business.

🛡️

We are not a payment intermediary

Your customers pay straight into your own payment gateway account, through your own API keys — the money goes directly to you. ZanWIFI Connect never receives, holds or touches it; we only verify that a payment succeeded so we can unlock internet access automatically.

Onboarding requirements

Three things to check off before going live.

📡Required

MikroTik or any RADIUS router

ZanWIFI Connect connects directly to your MikroTik to manage hotspot sessions in real time (RouterOS 6/7). Also works with any RADIUS-compatible router.

📲Recommended

Payment gateway account

Works with AzamPay, ClickPesa, PalmPesa, and more — your own account, your own money, zero delay.

💬Optional

SMS or WhatsApp

Send payment confirmations and expiry reminders to your customers directly.

ZanWIFI Connect is a cloud-based platform built specifically for MikroTik hotspot environments — perfect for hotels, student hostels, bus stations, markets, cafés, and independent WiFi entrepreneurs in Zanzibar and Tanzania.
